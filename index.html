How it all began

Our Story

How we met
The Beginning
It all started 2019 at ScaleFocus – with a handshake and a feeling we couldn't explain. It turned out that sometimes the heart knows before we do.
First adventures
First Trip Together after Covid
After months of long-distance conversations, I simply decided to go to Burgas. We wandered along the seaside as ‘just friends,’ without promises - but deep down, we both knew it wasn’t that simple.
Growing together
When you and me became we
A few weeks later, one weekend in Velingrad made everything feel natural. No big words, no official declarations - from September 11th, 2020, we were simply ‘us.’
Growing together
Moving In
Moving in together was a process too. Little by little, step by step, after months of ‘I’m just staying for a few days,’ by the beginning of summer 2023 his home had officially become ours.
A moment to remember
Our Furry Princess
Life sometimes gave us unpleasant surprises, but it always made up for them with something wonderful — our little princess Amy, the third member of our still-small family.
Adventures abroad
Travels & Memories
Travel reminds us that we’re alive. Over a few years, we explored over 20 countries across Europe and Asia, tasting new foods and talking to locals, and before that we wandered through the beauty of Bulgaria.
The question
The Proposal
On March 29th, 2025, in Athens, near the sea and the little church where we’ll be getting married, Niki asked, ‘Will you marry me?’. After 15 minutes of tears, laughter, and questions like ‘Are you serious?’, the most certain ‘Yes’ was finally said. And so began our next adventure together…
